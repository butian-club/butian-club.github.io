This is the first note on the Butian Engineering Club website. We want this site to make our work easier to understand and pass on.

{/* truncate */}

## What belongs here

- **Activity records:** notes from projects, competitions, tests and reviews.
- **Knowledge base:** tools, working methods and lessons collected in the [docs](/en/docs/intro).

## Why write it down?

Spoken explanations can disappear when members graduate. A clear record can be picked up by the next group. Writing down the work lets it keep helping people.
