At the **13–17 August 2025** GFSSM in Beijing, Butian's **Tianshu** and **Tianquan** teams advanced to the national final. Tianshu also earned **Best Qualifying Proposal**. The scenario placed a Mars base deep inside a lava tube in the year 2075.

## Designing for a Martian lava tube

The imagined base sat tens of metres below the surface. The brief included an algae factory, mineral extraction and production of carbon fibre from the atmosphere. Those ambitions needed to be tied to a defensible plan for habitat, infrastructure, structure and operations.

## Two companies on the podium

| Team | Virtual company | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tianshu | Orbital Industries | Runner-up |
| Tianquan | Jinwu Energy | Third place |

Tianshu member **Wu Yucheng** became company president and received the Best Leader award. **Shen Yue** was vice president of business, **Gu Chong** directed structure and **Chen Ran** directed infrastructure. In Tianquan's company, **Huang Guandiyang** was vice president of business and won Best Speaker; **Zhao Yihan** directed structure.

## The 24-hour design challenge

New members began with practice briefs and help from older club members. At the final, students ran for company roles and rehearsed their defense. At **8 a.m. on 15 August**, the companies received the 24-hour challenge. Early confusion, partial redesigns, overnight shifts and final checks all became part of the design process.
