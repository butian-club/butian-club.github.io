# Homepage departure imagery

- `hangzhou-no2-campus.webp`: crop and WebP conversion of Huandy618's photograph of a Hangzhou No.2 High School gate, 24 February 2025. [Wikimedia Commons source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Hangzhou_No._2_High_School_03.jpg), CC0 1.0.
- `earth-map.webp`: reduced WebP conversion of NASA Earth Observatory's July 2004 Blue Marble: Next Generation global base map. [NASA source](https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/blue-marble-next-generation/base-map/).
- `cloud-bank.webp`: purpose-made isolated cloud texture used on several spatial layers. It represents an atmospheric passage, not a photograph of a specific flight.
